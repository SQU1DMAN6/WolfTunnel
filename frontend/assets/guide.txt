1. Start a node using its existing protected state directory.
2. Ask its owner for one private invitation over a trusted channel.
3. In Settings, paste the invitation into the masked field; choose Connect.
4. Keys and credentials are generated and saved automatically.
5. Existing installations reconnect with their saved key on startup.
6. Manual setup supports older nodes: address, owner-verified pin and code.
7. A pin verifies the node; a new installation still needs owner approval.
8. Enter an HTTP/HTTPS URL, or preview a search and choose Go again.
9. Webpages execute on the node; the client displays pixels. Back/Forward navigate remote history. Closing a tab releases its page.
Ctrl/Cmd+T: new tab; W: close; L: navigation; comma: Settings; Q: quit.
Diagnostics shows logs and credential details. Scroll for smaller windows.
Operation and recovery guide: USAGE.md and docs/THREAT_MODEL-v0_5.md in the source.
